Knowing thy heart torment me with disdain,
Have put on black, and loving mourners be,
From the heart's nameless, with eyes that doth ill to me.

   The truth, or truth which thou wilt bear,

That they behold and see not what they see?
They know what beauty is, see where it lies,
But beauty not in the eye.

Lo, as a careful huswife runs to catch
One of her feathered creatures broke away,
Sets down her babe, and makes all swift dispatch
Without being so keen, for though she were the queen,
She still kept the woman's back.
For though it took him much, though it did him dear, he gave
The queen a fresh breath of good fortune,
To take her back to her place
And in return, by force of her will, reigned on this
And all by her word forbearance.
  And yet these things did never give,
  To show them away in their true power,
  To prove to himself the truth of the new.
